# Privacy Policy

**BoldVoice Vowel Progress Coach**

**Effective date:** 2026-08-08

BoldVoice Vowel Progress Coach is an unofficial browser extension for practicing pronunciation on the BoldVoice Vowel Map. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced by BoldVoice.

## Information Handled

The extension temporarily reads the vowel focus and anchor information displayed by the BoldVoice Vowel Map. This information is used to show live feedback and grade the practice item currently selected by the user.

The extension stores local training information, including the selected sound, curriculum position, practice attempts, correct and incorrect results, accuracy, skips, timestamps, daily summaries, and settings.

The extension does not record or store microphone audio, use a camera, read credentials or cookies, collect general browsing history, or collect personal communications, payment information, or personally identifiable information.

## How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide the extension's pronunciation-training features:

- Show the currently focused vowel and live calibration feedback.
- Grade practice attempts and calculate accuracy and coverage.
- Resume training from the previous position.
- Display local progress history and statistics.

## Storage And Sharing

Training information is stored locally using Chrome's local extension storage. If that storage API is unavailable, the extension may use browser local storage as a fallback. The extension does not maintain a server-side copy of training information.

The extension does not sell, share, or transfer training information to the publisher, advertisers, analytics providers, or other third parties. It does not use training information for advertising or profiling, and it does not allow people to read users' training information.

The page detector may request anchor data from the current BoldVoice page so it can map the page's chart coordinates. It does not include the user's training progress, microphone audio, or personal information in that request.

## Retention And Deletion

Information remains in browser storage until the user clears the extension or site data, or removes the extension. The extension has no server-side retention period because it does not upload training information.

## Changes

If the extension's data practices change, this policy and the in-product disclosure will be updated before the changed practice is used.

## Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the support contact published on the Chrome Web Store listing for this extension.

## Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from the BoldVoice Vowel Map and Chrome storage is limited to providing and improving the extension's single purpose and complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
